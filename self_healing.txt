Step 1: Verify the current deployment 
            kubectl get deployments 
            kubectl get pods 
Step 2: Scale to three replicas 
            kubectl scale deployment student-api --replicas=3 
            kubectl get pods 
 
Three Student API pods are created and reach Running. 
Step 3: Demonstrate self-healing 
Copy the name of one Student API pod: 
            kubectl get pods 
Delete that specific pod: 
            kubectl delete pod YOUR_POD_NAME 
Check again: 
            kubectl get pods 
Kubernetes should create a replacement pod to maintain the desired replica count.

Step 4: Restart the Deployment 
kubectl rollout restart deployment student-api  
kubectl rollout status deployment student-api 
Step 5: Restore one replica 
kubectl scale deployment student-api --replicas=1 
kubectl get pods 
Wait until the terminating pods disappear and one pod remains Running.
